
The Two Seeds
Two seeds lay side by side in the rich soil of a quiet garden.
The first seed felt afraid of the dark and the unknown. "If I push my roots down, I might hit hard rocks," it worried. "If I push my shoot up, someone might step on me." So the first seed stayed still and waited.
The second seed wanted to grow. It pushed its tiny roots into the earth and stretched its green shoot toward the warm sun. Soon, the wind blew hard, and heavy rain poured down. The little sprout bent and shivered, yet it held on.
Spring arrived, and the second seed bloomed into a bright yellow flower that brought joy to the whole garden. Meanwhile, the first seed never opened and remained buried in the dirt.
Moral: Growth requires courage. Stepping out of your comfort zone helps you reach your potential.
