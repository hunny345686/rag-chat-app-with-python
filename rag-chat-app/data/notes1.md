
The Cracked Pot

A water bearer carried two large pots each day from a stream to his master’s house. One pot was whole and delivered a full portion of water, while the other had a small crack and arrived only half full.
For two years, the cracked pot felt ashamed of its flaw. One day, it spoke to the water bearer by the stream. "I am sorry I leak," it said. "Because of my crack, you only get half the water you work so hard to carry."
The water bearer smiled gently and pointed to the path they walked every day. "Look at the flowers growing on your side of the trail," he said. "Knowing about your flaw, I planted flower seeds along your side. Every day as we walked back, you watered them, and now I can pick these beautiful flowers to brighten my master's table."
Moral: Your flaws do not make you useless. What you see as a weakness can bring unexpected beauty and value to the world.
